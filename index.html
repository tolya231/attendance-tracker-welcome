🎉 Attendance Tracker Installed!

Two quick steps and your first roll call is ready.

1. Pin the extension

Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin Attendance Tracker so it is always one click away.

2. Take your first attendance

Open a Google Meet call with the participant panel visible, click the icon and press Start capture. Everyone in the call lands in a dated session you can export to CSV or paste into Google Sheets.
